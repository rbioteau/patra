# The launch animation

*Loaded when work touches `lib/src/features/launch/`. The root `CLAUDE.md` stays the entry point and carries the rules that apply everywhere; this file holds the argument behind this directory alone.*

The app opens by assembling its own logo. The animation is the brand kit's, transcribed into `lib/src/branding/patra_launch.dart` — a `_LaunchPainter` that is a function of authored time only, so the timeline can be read without a widget tree. **4.8s**: the first glyph cluster drifts in from the upper left (0.10–2.50s), the second from the upper right (0.55–2.95s), the Latin signature rises under them (2.40–3.20s), the assembled word holds (2.95–4.05s), then it fades into the app (4.05–4.80s). The numbers are the kit's, not ours to round.

What drifts in is **one glyph cluster each**, not the whole word: `PatraLogoPaths.leaf1` and `leaf2` are the two halves of पत्र, and each is given its own origin, its own sway (`sin` terms on x, y, rotation and a `rotateY` tilt through a perspective of 1/700) and its own damping (`r²`, where `r = 1 - t`), so they arrive without ever being a sliding word. Past 2950ms both are drawn in one coordinate system, which is what makes the shirorekha — the headline across the top — join exactly rather than meeting at a seam.

`LaunchAnimation` wraps the **whole app** from `MaterialApp.builder` rather than being a route, so the app is mounted, laid out and fetching underneath from the first frame: what the fade reveals is the real screen rather than a skeleton appearing behind it. Three consequences worth knowing:

- **Whether there is anything to play is decided before the first build.** `_play` is set in `didChangeDependencies`, which runs before `build`, and `build` returns the child outright when it is false. The kit deferred the whole decision to a post-frame callback, which meant a splash that mounted and then took itself away — one frame of ink over whoever the app had just been handed to.
- **The ground is the page's own colour** (`patraBg`), not a separate brand tile. The old mark was grown *out of* an ink a shade warmer than the app, so the handoff read as a lift; the new one is assembled out of the background the app itself draws, and a window a shade off it would read as a cut. That is also why a test cannot find the splash by its colour and looks for `patraLaunchSplashKey` instead.
- **Nothing flies.** The old animation measured `LaunchLogoSlot`s and landed the frond on the header's; the word simply fades where it stands, so there is no registry, no measured rects, and no screen that has to offer a slot — the header, the two gate screens and a linked screen are all treated the same, which is one less thing for a new screen to know.

A tap sends the splash home — it seeks to the fade and lands, rather than cutting the animation out from under the app (`skip()`, exposed for exactly that). `MediaQuery.disableAnimations` skips it outright: someone who asked the OS for less motion is not asking to watch a logo assemble itself. The splash is created once, at app start, and takes itself out of the tree when done: a resume is not a launch. **Nor is a handover**, and that one had to be said in a provider rather than left to the tree: entering another profile builds the whole app again (`SessionScope`), so the animation would be mounted a second time and assemble the word over somebody who has just tapped their own face. `isLaunchProvider` is false in every container after the first, and `LaunchAnimation` is what reads it.

`lib/src/branding/patra_mark.dart` is the mark itself, shared by the header and the two gate screens, drawing the same two outlines the animation composes. `PatraMark` is sized by the **word** — measured off the union of the two paths, because the 150-unit box the kit draws in reserves two and a half times the height the word actually occupies, which in the home header is the difference between a 24pt mark and a 57pt hole.

The **gate screens carry the same lockup** at their own scale — mark `40 × 24/22` — so the two are the same thing at two sizes rather than two arrangements. It is one widget (`PatraMasthead`), because the picker and the sign-in form are seen back to back and spacing that drifted between them would show. Its gap is the one number that does *not* scale: 0.65em against the header's 0.81em, because spacing that reads as one lockup at 22pt opens into a gulf when the same ratio is taken to 40pt. What is being held constant is that the word and the signature read as one thing, not the ratio that achieves it.

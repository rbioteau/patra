One-finger zoom: does this gesture keep the page whole?

Pinch is fiddly one-handed, so the reader would zoom with a single finger drag: the point you press becomes a reference point, the page follows your finger — pull down and the part above your finger comes into view, pull right and the part to the left does — and how far you drag decides how much it magnifies. The question this demo answers is the one that only shows up at the edges: when the drag asks for something the page cannot give — you press near the top and pull down, but there is nothing above to reveal — what should give way? Four answers are wired up below — three that drag the page and one that does not drag it at all. Drag the page around and watch the state; the edge of the page is drawn as a bright purple frame, so it is obvious the moment the page stops covering the screen.

# Patra screens today

Screenshots of the app on an Android phone (1080x2414), in French, over a real library; the profile photo and the server address were replaced. They are the reference for layout, density and rhythm: match them when designing a screen that exists, and extend them when designing a new one. They are not images to place in a design.

- `screens/01-home.png`: Home. The resume hero (cover, serif title, gold Continue button) under the app bar with the mark, the "À suivre" shelf of covers, the library cards.
- `screens/02-library.png`: the library grid with its type filter pills.
- `screens/03-series.png`: a series. The hero, the "Download what's next" batch card, the reading-position groups ("En cours", "À suivre"), chapter rows with their gold progress track.
- `screens/04-series-list.png`: the same list further down: Save pills, the folded "Déjà lus · 2 — Afficher".
- `screens/05-reader-manga.png`: a page of pictures on black, no chrome.
- `screens/06-reader-chrome.png`: the same page with its chrome: title bar and cog, the thumbnail strip, the gold slider, "101 / 188" in the serif.
- `screens/08-reader-settings.png`: the reader's cog sheet: reading direction, the chosen one ticked in gold.
- `screens/09-downloads.png`: the Downloads tab, empty.
- `screens/10-settings.png` and `screens/11-settings-storage.png`: Settings: the profile card, the lock, language, storage and about.
- `screens/12-profiles.png`: the profile picker ("Qui lit ?") with the lockup.
